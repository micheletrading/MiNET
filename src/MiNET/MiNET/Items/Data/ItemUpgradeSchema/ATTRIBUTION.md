# With thanks to Dylan T. and PocketMine-MP

The 27 schema files here are copied verbatim from
[pmmp/BedrockItemUpgradeSchema](https://github.com/pmmp/BedrockItemUpgradeSchema), maintained by
[Dylan T. (dktapps)](https://github.com/dktapps). They are what lets a chest saved in 2017 still
hand a player the item that was put in it.

The full credit, and what it took to produce this data, is written up next to its sibling set in
[Blocks/Data/BlockStateUpgradeSchema/ATTRIBUTION.md](../../../Blocks/Data/BlockStateUpgradeSchema/ATTRIBUTION.md).

Released under CC0 1.0, so nothing is required of us. Keep the files byte-identical to upstream and
take new versions from the repository rather than editing them here.
